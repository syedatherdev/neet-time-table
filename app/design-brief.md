# NEAT NEET Planner — design brief

- **Design read:** A focused, optimistic command desk for a student balancing coaching, four subjects, and a dense exam calendar; it should feel calm, accountable, and motivating rather than like a generic productivity SaaS.
- **Concept spine:** The Study Expedition. The student moves through a sequence of carefully marked waypoints from today's session to the December finish line. Every chapter is a checkpoint on the same route.
- **Delivery tier:** cinema. The home page is a cinematic scroll journey with a usable study planner immediately following it.
- **Locked palette:** deep botanical green `#173D35`, bright paper `#F4F2E9`, ink `#172B27`, muted sage `#A9B8A7`, and a single restrained vermilion-pink accent `#D66B68`. The green/paper pairing connects to focused study and printed revision notes; the rose accent is reserved for deadlines and active progress.
- **Locked type:** Satoshi-style expressive grotesk for display, Geist for interface and reading, Geist Mono for dates, times, and progress values.
Animation mode: animated-website
- **Journey shape:** single-shot. One continuous macro-to-wide camera move over a tactile study expedition still life gives a coherent narrative without scene seams.
- **Journey:** 1) The first mark: an open annotated notebook and sharpened pencil, headline “Make every study hour count.” 2) The route: a paper calendar with marked milestones, headline “One clear path to December.” 3) The four disciplines: four colored subject tabs in a single bound study atlas, headline “Four subjects. One steady rhythm.” 4) The finish line: a completed revision page beside a quiet clock, headline “Arrive ready on 27 December.”
- **World grammar:** tactile editorial tabletop photographed from a shallow overhead angle, deep botanical green and warm paper with restrained rose marks, soft directional daylight from upper left, graphite pencil details, subtle paper grain, quiet premium academic still-life, one continuous slow camera push and orbit, no cuts or text in footage.
- **Mobile framing:** all hero objects centered in the central 60% safe zone, readable at 9:16 crop; mobile uses a lighter encode and same center-safe focal point.
- **Delivery budget:** desktop film ≤32 MiB, mobile film ≤16 MiB.
- **Section plan:** hero journey; asymmetric overview dashboard; wide daily timetable; horizontal date rail for the exam calendar; stacked subject syllabus with filters; final reflection/revision checkpoint. Each uses a distinct layout family.
- **Asset plan:** continuous hero film and exact-frame desktop/mobile posters; two editorial study-detail plates; one generated 8-glyph icon sheet; a monogram/wordmark family; launch cover/OG and favicon set.
- **CTA inventory:** “Open today’s plan” (primary hero action), “View exam route” (underlined route link), “Mark session done” (compact checkpoint action), “Open syllabus” (subject-specific disclosure), “Open planner” (final return action).
- **Product behavior:** interactive daily plan, date navigation, subject syllabus checklists, progress and completion state, exam countdown, search/filter, and browser-local persistence for the student's own progress. All content is grounded in the supplied exam image and the requested daily time windows.
